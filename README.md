# MicroZig — LLMs Are Tools Fork

> **Why this fork exists**
>
> This fork of [MicroZig](https://github.com/ZigEmbeddedGroup/microzig) exists because upstream now rules on *how* a contribution was written, not only on whether it works.
>
> On October 28, 2026, the ZigEmbeddedGroup adopted a "Strict No LLM / No AI" policy. It doesn't stop at AI-generated code. It bans using an LLM to fix your spelling, to translate your words, to brainstorm, or to find bugs, and it bans even *talking* about these tools. Under this policy, a working, tested contribution can be thrown out not because of anything wrong with it, but because of the tool that was open on the contributor's screen.
>
> We don't think that's a quality standard. A rule that forbids discussing a tool doesn't protect a community; it polices it. Contributors who use modern tools are treated as suspects, people writing in a second language can't use a tool to polish their English before posting, and anyone who simply works differently is shut out.
>
> We see it differently. LLMs are tools, like compilers, linters, debuggers and search engines. They aren't a threat to the craft. Open source works best when it judges the work, not the worker's toolbox.
>
> Here, contributions are judged on their merits and nothing else. See our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for the full policy.
>
> This fork tracks upstream, and credit for MicroZig belongs to its authors. Our door stays open: if the ZigEmbeddedGroup's policy changes, we'll gladly send our work back.
